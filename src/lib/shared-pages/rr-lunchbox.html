Ask the landlord or old owner

If closing is already done, ask anyway. These are the things that cost money or weeks after you move in.

Can the health permit transfer?

No, the food permit doesn't transfer to a new owner, so R & R Lunchbox applies new. It's much easier than a brand new restaurant though: the kitchen is already built, so it's mostly a menu and equipment review plus a pre-opening inspection. The health permit also moved from DHEC to the SC Department of Agriculture, so apply through SCDA Retail Food Safety.

The beer and wine permit is where the old restaurant helps: if their permit is still valid, you can get a temporary permit to sell for up to 120 days while yours processes.
